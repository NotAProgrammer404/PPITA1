# Adsorption:
Accumulation of molecular species of the surface rather than bulk of solid or liquid.

![Image: A diagram illustrating adsorption. Gas (adsorbate) particles are shown moving towards and accumulating on a surface (adsorbent).](image_placeholder.jpg)
`→ Gas (adsorbate)`
`→ surface (Adsorbent)`

*   #Entropy (ΔS) = -ve
    `ΔH > TΔS`
*   #Gibbs free energy (ΔG) = -ve

# Desorption:
The process of removing adsorbate from adsorbent.

# Absorption:
when atom/molecule/ion entre the bulk of solid or liquid.
eg. H₂O (vap) absorbed by CaCl₂

# Sorption:
adsorption & absorption Simultaneously
eg. Dying of fabric.

# Type of adsorption:

**Physical adsorption**
*   Van der waal's force
*   Reversible force
*   low activation energy
*   It is not specific

**Chemical adsorption**
*   covalent force
*   Irreversible
*   high activation energy
*   Highly specific.

`easily liquifiable gas`
`form more physisorption`

`# Physical adsorption ∝ α ∝ Tc α liquification`

`# Tc = 8a/27Rb => Tc ∝ α`

# Factors affecting adsorption
1.  Nature of gas : Adsorption ∝ ΔTc
2.  Nature of adsorbent: adsorbent (activated) {adsorbent}
3.  Specific area of solid: adsorption ∝ surface area

# Effect of pressure on gas
*   Adsorption increase with increase in pressure.

# Effect of temp on adsorption
Physisorption ∝ 1/temp
Chemisorption ∝ temp ↓
